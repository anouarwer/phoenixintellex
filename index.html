03 — COVERAGE

Six asset classes. One engine.

RESIDENTIAL

Mortgage loans secured on residential property. Recovery accounts for judicial enforcement timelines and forced-sale discounts by jurisdiction.

COMMERCIAL (CRE)

Income-producing commercial assets. Recovery anchored to stressed capitalisation rates. Loan concentration and tenant quality factored.

SME / CORPORATE

Small and medium enterprise loans. CDR differentiated by country macro conditions and sector NACE codes. Unsecured portion modeled separately.

UNSECURED

Consumer credit and unsecured loans. Highest CDR segment. Recovery driven purely by servicing strategy and legal jurisdiction efficiency.

LAND

Undeveloped land with no income stream. Recovery based on forced-sale values with disposal discounts. Highest correlation sensitivity.

DEVELOPER LOANS
